The root cause of this is that the slashing mechanism only takes into account the activeStake of a staker while deciding to slash a user, while the rewards mechanism takes both activeStake and pendingStake into account. Update: Fixed as of commit fd8cecc6719a7a69d44287b2096866686313593c of pull request #4168 . Then the price request can only be resolved if another voter who staked before the request participates once the request rolls over again.
